## Why ElfHosted instead of self-hosting or another host?

I chose elf hosted because it supports the devs with part of the subscription and they are part of the community itself so they understand fully what’s going on on updates and everything. They handle all the updates and figure out what works best for what the users mainly use it for. I don’t have to worry about using AI to figure out how to do updates and fix what’s broken. The community is also very helpful on what works and doesn’t.

**When did you move to ElfHosted, and what prompted it?**

I moved about five months ago. I was self hosting, but then some stuff got messed up on my server and I got frustrated. Trying to fix it for what I was spending to always have stuff running.

**How much time did your old setup take each month?**

It would generally take about an hour and a half a week

## Is ElfHosted what I expected?

I was worried about indexers’s not working with it properly. Which has not been a single. I didn’t really have any expectations when I started besides knowing that it had some really good reviews.

**Which part of getting started on ElfHosted took longest?**

Figuring out how to get some stuff activated. I wasn’t quite sure what I was looking at at times and how to get there when there would be update updates with AI streams. But I was able to find stuff in the community or on the Guide

**How long did it take to get your first app running on ElfHosted?**

Probably five minutes

Debridge works the best by far. It makes everything super simple.

**What do you no longer have to do since moving to ElfHosted?**

I no longer have to manually worry about finding updates. I also no longer have to worry about finding what is broken in my house set up and worrying if my server is gonna cut off at a random time.

## What goes wrong, and how does it get fixed?

Nothing has broke. But it has saved me many headaches from having to fix my own broke self hosted server

## Has it changed my free time or family life?

Lots of people now use it. And it’s also taught me a lot about how to handle setting up for other stuff. Now I can just plug and play unless I want to tinker with some setting roughly. So I don’t have to worry about messing with anything that I can’t just handle on my phone.

**How many hours a month does upkeep take now, compared with before ElfHosted?**

Maybe half an hour a month so I can just change settings and tinker for my own interest

**What do you do with the time you used to spend on upkeep?**

Play games or spend more time outside. Less troubleshooting.

## How useful is Sam, ElfHosted's AI assistant?

Sam has helped me run stuff to get credentials better which has allowed me to host my NNTP stuff. And connect different things to the server as needed.

## Is ElfHosted worth the money?

It is completely worth what I pay. I am incredibly happy with the value.

**Which plan or apps do you pay for?**

I pay for the 4K proxy with nzbdav subscription

**What hardware or upkeep did NZBDav on ElfHosted save you?**

I don’t have to worry about docker getting messed up or power shutting off so then everyone loses access to their streaming

## What are the downsides?

Honestly, for what it is, it does a spectacular job. My only suggestion would be for what I use currently which is the NC Devvy bundle. I don’t know exactly how I can connect certain things to it and what I can and can’t connect with my sub subscription. It’s not very clear what is locked and what isn’t

**Is there a workaround you use on ElfHosted for now?**

I just don’t use those functions. Which it’s only for my own interest in tinkering to use that. It doesn’t prevent me from doing anything that I would normally do anyways.

## Who is ElfHosted for, and who should skip it?

I would honestly suggest it to anyone who streams stuff. Especially with the Wizard option being available you can just basically pay to have it set up for you. It’s very simple for people to set up if they know what they’re looking at.

**What problem would ElfHosted solve for the kind of person it suits?**

It takes away all the worrying headache of issues happening and having to always worry about updates and figuring out what’s not working and why. You just get to use it and enjoy what it provides.

**Who would ElfHosted not suit, and why?**
